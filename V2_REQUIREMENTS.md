# Electricity library v2 acceptance criteria

User priority: PYQs first; review every available official PDF/set and extract every Electricity question, not a selected sample. Keep original source question images. Separate PYQ and SQP collections visibly. Produce substantial master question-bank PDFs, improve document layout and preview, retain original practice and detailed notes.

## Ownership
- research/pyq_v2: source-audit coordinator and its year workers; existing research immutable.
- site UI/docs/tests: interface developer; publish V2_CONTRACT.md early.
- assembly plus site/data/assets/research/downloads: assembler; stage before publication.
- Parent: cross-checks, release notes, Git/GitHub Pages, live verification.

## Completeness standard
Every inventoried physical PDF needs a ledger entry with official URL, archive member/paper code/year/exam, SHA256, total pages, actually reviewed pages, relevant questions, scope exclusions, duplicate/language status, and unresolved issues. Distinguish downloaded, text-extracted, fully reviewed and publication-verified. Do not certify a whole PDF from keyword filtering or a few reviewed pages. Accessibility alternatives and internal choices need accounting. Exact duplicate files can share audit evidence by hash with explicit linkage; translated variants cannot silently be treated as identical.

Missing official years or unreachable files are explicit retrieval gaps. Do not invent questions, provenance, original sections or marks. A complete question bank may contain distinct source occurrences with repeated wording; retain source identifiers and label repetition instead of silently dropping sets.

## Content and presentation
- PYQ-only master/volumes and year collections, SQP-only master/volumes or session collections; do not mix them under these labels.
- Existing 15 mixed compilations may remain clearly identified as mixed legacy/editorial practice; four originals and two notes retained.
- Preserve original crops with diagrams, full question context and exact branch labels. No unrelated magnetic material bleeding into crops.
- Source-backed metadata at collection time: section, printed question number, physical PDF page, marks, paper code, official URL, alternatives/choice groups, verification status.
- Correct Class X NCERT Electricity scope; no Class XII Current Electricity or magnetic-effects mechanisms.
- Large PDFs: readable image scaling, meaningful ordering/index/bookmarks, clickable source references, appendices, deliberate pagination. No arbitrary fixed question limit.
- Website: straightforward PYQ/SQP navigation, year/set filter, coverage ledger, responsive zoomable evidence images and PDF preview with fallback.

## Release
Tests and strict source/data validation must pass. Check representative long PDF pages and live downloads after deployment. Preserve explicit answer holds. Never upgrade verification solely to satisfy a validator. Publish actual counts and remaining gaps; no unconditional claim that every historical CBSE paper exists in the collection.
